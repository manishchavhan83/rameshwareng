Full Lifecycle Tooling

Engineered for Extreme Precision & Durability

From 3D tooling simulation to final press tryouts, our Dehu facility handles high-tonnage tooling requirements with tight tolerance controls.

01 / DESIGN & SIMULATION

Tool & Die Engineering

Complete CAD/CAM 3D modeling, strip layout optimization, and press tool simulation to prevent split lines, wrinkles, or material thinning before machining.

02 / VMC MACHINING

High-Speed CNC Machining

Heavy-duty vertical machining centers (VMC) capable of cutting hardened tool steels, die blocks, and complex cavity profiles with sub-micron accuracy.

03 / PRESS TRYOUT & TESTING

Heavy Tryout Capacity

In-house press trial setups backed by heavy tonnage capabilities, enabling quick initial sample inspection (ISIR) and batch validation.

04 / JIGS & WELD FIXTURES

Precision Assembly Fixtures

Custom design and fabrication of modular welding, inspection, and machining fixtures for high-volume automotive production lines.

05 / HOT FORMING DIES

Specialized Casting & Dies

Development of robust hot forming and metal casting dies for 10mm to 16mm thick heavy structural components.

06 / TOOL MAINTENANCE

Re-sharpening & Refurbishment

Rapid repair, die grinding, re-sharpening, and modification services to extend the operational lifespan of existing customer tooling setup.
