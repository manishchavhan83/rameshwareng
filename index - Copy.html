About Rameshwar Group

A technology & engineering driven development partner

Founded in 1998 by Mr. Kailash Rahangdale, a technocrat with over 22 years of experience in the engineering & manufacturing industry, Rameshwar Engineers offers concept-to-product design services, engineering & manufacturing solutions, and testing & validation services.

We manufacture a wide range of high-performance, critical & safety components for the automotive and non-automotive sector — currently operating and expansion plant in place to meet global growth and customer requirements.

What We Do

Concept to product, under one roof

Design, tooling and manufacturing capability built to handle complex processes and components — matched by an expert technical team averaging 15 years of experience.

01

Design & R&D

Concept-to-product design services backed by engineering expertise across critical and safety components.

02

Press Tools & Dies

Cutting, forming, piercing, blanking and cam-pierce dies manufactured to tight tolerances, 10mm to 16mm thick.

03

Jig & Fixture Manufacturing

Precision jigs and fixtures engineered for repeatable, high-accuracy assembly and machining operations.

04

VMC Machining & Assembly

Multi-axis VMC machining from 1200×600×700 up to 2800×1800×1000 work envelopes, plus full assembly.

05

Hot Forming & Casting Dies

Hot forming die manufacturing and tooling from 10mm to 16mm thickness for high-strength housings.

06

Testing & Validation

Rigorous testing and validation services ensuring every critical and safety component meets export standards.
