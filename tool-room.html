Key Capabilities

Concept to finished tool, under one roof

Our expert tooling team utilizes an integration of advanced machining technologies and strict quality control to deliver high-performance tooling setups.

01 / ENGINEERING

Tool Design & CAD/CAM

Complete 3D tool modeling, simulation, and strip layout optimization using parametric CAD/CAM suites to ensure flawless manufacturing.

02 / MANUFACTURING

Dies & Molds Production

Fabrication of precision dies (progressive, compound, and stage dies) and complex casting molds utilizing multi-axis VMC machining.

03 / SPECIAL FIXTURES

Jigs & Fixtures Fabrication

Custom design and fabrication of modular welding jigs, assembly fixtures, and inspection gauges for repeatable production accuracy.

04 / VALIDATION

Tool Tryouts & Validation

Integrated tryout facility with up to heavy tonnage press capacity to validate tool performance and produce Initial Sample Inspection Reports (ISIR).

05 / MAINTENANCE

Refurbishment & Repair

Comprehensive preventive maintenance, tool sharpening, damage repair, and modification services to extend tool operational lifespan.
