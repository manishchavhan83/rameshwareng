Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our manufacturing infrastructure, tooling capabilities, corporate entities, and project lead times.

Have another question?
What specific components and tools do you specialize in?

We offer comprehensive concept-to-product design, engineering, and testing services. Our manufacturing core specialties include:

  • High-performance press tools, dies, and jig fixtures.
  • Precision multi-axis VMC machining.
  • Hot forming and casting dies ranging from 10mm to 16mm in thickness.
  • Critical safety and automotive components, notably rear axle and banjo housings.
How are Rameshwar Engineers, Onkar Dies, and Tusshar Axles connected?

Rameshwar Engineers operates as part of the unified Rameshwar Group entity, which strategically comprises both Onkar Dies Pvt. Ltd. and Tusshar Axles Pvt. Ltd. to pool specialized tooling and engineering capabilities under one robust umbrella.

What is your production and press facility capacity?

Our heavy tooling operations are supported by a 12,000 sq ft built-up manufacturing facility in Dehugaon, Pune, an advanced tryout area equipped with a 10-ton overhead crane, and up to a heavy 1000-ton press capacity situated at Bhamboli, Chakan.

Do you manufacture for markets outside of Pune?

Yes. While our primary production units are anchored in Pune, we manufacture and validate tools to global export standards. We consistently supply critical components and tooling setups to key industrial automotive hubs across India, including Chennai, Haryana, and Aurangabad.

What is your typical lead time for custom tool or die manufacturing?

Lead times vary depending entirely on design complexity, part geometry, simulation feedback, and material specifications. Once we review your initial component drawings and requirements, our technical team provides a definitive development timeline spanning raw design to the final trial validation run.
